Three-Variable Charts

Pick a dataset and up to three variables. On a chart they become the x-axis, the y-axis, and colour. On the map they become location, shading, and points.

Regional El Niño patterns shape wheat-flour price responses to climate disasters. Environmental Research Letters 21, 184039 (replication data).
Data sources. Disasters: EM-DAT, the International Disaster Database (CRED, UCLouvain). Conflict: UCDP Georeferenced Event Dataset (Uppsala Conflict Data Program). Food prices: World Food Programme, Vulnerability Analysis and Mapping. El Niño: NOAA Climate Prediction Center, Oceanic Niño Index. Map outlines: Natural Earth, drawn with Plotly.
